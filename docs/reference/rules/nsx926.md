## What it checks

`--model` names no Gold Model in the project, or a name more than one Gold Model has.

## Why

`--model` picks a Gold Model by its name, such as `fct_orders`, or by its Logical Name, such as `sales.gold.fct_orders`. Two Domains, or two model Layers of one Domain, can each have a model of one name, in its own schema. Such a name could mean either, so Nimblestax doesn't guess. `nsx test` and `nsx compile` both take `--model`, and each exits 2, before reaching the workspace.

## Example

In a project, this fails:

```console
$ nsx test --model fct_order
```

## Fix

Give a Gold Model's name, or its Logical Name when more than one model has the name. The diagnostic lists the ones it could be.
